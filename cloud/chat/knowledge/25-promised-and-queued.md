# What was promised, and where the queue stands now

## 1. Prevailing-wage levels on H-1B filings

Every H-1B filing names a wage level for the occupation: Level I is the entry
rate, Level IV the experienced one.

| Level | Filings | Share |
|---|---|---|
| Level I | 180,331 | 15.7% |
| Level II | 563,090 | 49.0% |
| Level III | 242,975 | 21.1% |
| Level IV | 163,711 | 14.2% |

**64.6% of certified filings name Level I or II**, across
1,150,107 filings in 2019 and 2023.

| Largest filers | Certified filings | Level I or II |
|---|---|---|
| Cognizant Technology Solutions Us Corp | 43,264 | **50.7%** |
| Infosys Limited | 30,496 | **71.2%** |
| Tata Consultancy Services Limited | 21,903 | **86.2%** |
| Ernst & Young U.S. Llp | 21,087 | **60.0%** |
| Google Llc | 18,943 | **61.3%** |
| Amazon.Com Services Llc | 15,646 | **70.6%** |
| Microsoft Corporation | 15,120 | **56.3%** |
| Capgemini America Inc | 11,388 | **56.6%** |
| Deloitte Consulting Llp | 11,178 | **69.1%** |
| Accenture Llp | 9,575 | **43.9%** |

**Filing low is lawful.** It is the cheapest legal way to fill a role, and this
measures what was *promised*, not what was paid.

**The obvious follow-up cannot be answered.** Whether low wage levels predict
later violations is not testable here: only six of these employers appear in
the published enforcement extract, which carries only its largest cases, so
absence is not evidence of no findings. Enforcement is also not random — large
employers and staffing firms are investigated more, and both skew low-level.
Six matches support no correlation in either direction. Say that plainly if
asked.

## 2. The queue, now current to 2026

The historical charts stop at FY2023; the monthly bulletins carry it forward.
India, EB-2, most recent months:

| Month | Priority date being acted on | Years waited |
|---|---|---|
| 2023-09 | 2011-01-01 | 12.7 |
| 2026-05 | 2014-07-15 | 11.8 |
| 2026-06 | 2013-09-01 | 12.7 |
| 2026-07 | U | — |
| 2026-08 | U | — |

In August 2026 the position is stark and worth stating exactly: **India's EB-2
is "U" — unavailable**, while all other chargeability areas are **"C" —
current, no queue at all.** "U" is a wait of unknown length, **not** a wait of
zero, and must never be averaged in as a number.

India also *retrogressed* during 2026: the date acted on moved backwards from
15 July 2014 in May to 1 September 2013 in June. The queue can move in both
directions.
