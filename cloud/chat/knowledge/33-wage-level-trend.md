# Which way the wage level is moving

## The trend runs opposite to the snapshot

The wage-level finding rested on two years. Read across the whole disclosure
archive, the share at the bottom two levels has **fallen**:

| Fiscal year | Certified filings | Level I | Level I or II |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2017 | 470,084 | 44.4% | 80.5% |
| 2018 | 576,359 | 17.9% | 72.1% |
| 2019 | 567,880 | 15.0% | 69.0% |
| 2020 | 523,135 | 14.0% | 64.2% |
| 2021 | 703,998 | 15.3% | 62.7% |
| 2022 | 570,524 | 14.8% | 59.7% |
| 2023 | 582,227 | 16.4% | 60.4% |
| 2024 | 493,324 | 19.8% | 64.0% |
| 2025 | 524,350 | 20.8% | 62.7% |

**80.5% in 2017 to 62.7% in 2025**,
and Level I alone from 44.4% to 20.8%.
Employers file **higher** on the legal scale than they did. Say so wherever the
level figures appear — it is the one measure in this project moving in the
direction its critics would want.

The break is a single year: Level I falls 44.4% to
17.9% between 2017 and 2018, the year after
an executive order directed agencies to scrutinise H-1B wage levels. Both
files use the same column and the same visa-class field, so it is a real
reading and not a recording change. **A coincidence in time is not a
demonstrated cause.**

## This does not soften the other finding

Two different questions, and both answers stand:

- **The level mix has improved.** Employers file higher on the scale.
- **The scale itself sits low against local pay.** 84.0% of filings still
  promise below the median for their occupation in the metro where the work is.

Moving from Level I to Level II is movement within a range whose bottom three
rungs mostly sit under the local median. Do not let one finding be used to
retire the other.

## Limits

- **Pre-FY2017 files carry no wage-level column**, so the series cannot start
  earlier. FY2026 is partial and excluded from comparisons.
- **FY2018 ships two overlapping annual releases.** Summing them doubled the
  year to 939,820 against a true 576,359; the larger is kept and the discarded
  one is recorded in `superseded_duplicates`.
- What was **promised**, not paid. Filing at any level is lawful.
