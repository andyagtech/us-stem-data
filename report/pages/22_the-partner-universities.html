10. The partner universities

Where the Build Fellowship says it operates

The organisation publishes 54 university partners across 20 states. Joined to IPEDS, those institutions conferred 109,643 non-resident graduate STEM degrees between 2021 and 2025.

A partnership is the organisation’s own claim that it trains students at that institution. It does not mean the university sponsors anyone, employs a fellow, or is party to any petition — the affiliation that confers cap exemption is the nonprofit’s.
