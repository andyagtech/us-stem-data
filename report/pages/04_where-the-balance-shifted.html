3. Where the balance shifted

Across thirty years the shift is at master’s level, and it is now approaching half of all core-STEM master’s degrees

The doctorate is the smaller end of graduate education and the one most often measured. Taken across every year IPEDS supports, the master’s degree moved more than four times as far.

A separate collection reaches further back and is restricted to science, engineering and health. On it, the temporary-visa share of graduate enrolment went from 13.7% in 1980 to 38.5%, and among postdoctoral researchers from 35.4% to 58.3% — a majority of the people doing the country’s postdoctoral science.

Domestic output rose at both levels over the period — master’s +189%, doctorate +74% — so this is a change in share, not an American retreat. Non-resident is a visa category: a permanent resident is counted under their race and appears here as domestic, so these lines understate the foreign-born share and say nothing about where the visa holders end up.
