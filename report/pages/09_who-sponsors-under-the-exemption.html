Why the industry code cannot be used instead

Counting employers filing under Educational Services fails: the codes are self-reported and often describe something other than the institution, so major research employers are absent from that category entirely.

The two approaches disagree in both directions: the directories name 130 employers the industry code misses, and the code captures 3,075 the directories exclude — largely school districts and for-profit colleges, which are not cap-exempt. That two independent proxies disagree this sharply is why the exemption cannot be measured from published data.
