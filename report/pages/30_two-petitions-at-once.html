11. Two petitions at once

The employers hosting fellows file concurrent petitions at 33 times the rate

The arrangement requires a worker to hold two H-1B petitions simultaneously: a cap-exempt one through the nonprofit, and one at the company where the work is done. USCIS reports concurrent petitions as their own category.

No source links a named worker to a petition, and a concurrent approval at one of these employers is not evidence that a fellow holds it. Establishing that would need the cap status USCIS declines to publish.
