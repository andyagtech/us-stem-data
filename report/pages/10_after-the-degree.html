5. After the degree

The pipeline does not end at graduation: work authorisation has grown faster than the degrees

Optional Practical Training lets a graduate work in the United States on a student visa — twelve months for any field, and a further twenty-four for STEM graduates. The STEM extension dates from 2008: it accounted for 2 authorisations in 2007 and 95,384 in 2024.

OPT is the bridge between the degree and the H-1B. It rests on DHS regulation rather than statute, and is not subject to the H-1B numerical cap. It also carries a payroll-tax exemption that an American graduate competing for the same job does not — page {{page:fica}}.
