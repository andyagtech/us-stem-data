9. What enforcement finds

The Labor Department has recovered $123 million in unpaid H-1B wages — and it looks less often than it used to

Every survey in this document reaches doctorate holders. The H-1B workforce mostly holds a bachelor’s or master’s and works in industry, and no survey here reaches it. Enforcement records do: one row per investigation, with the employer named.

Read this as a floor, never a rate. These are violations that were found, which requires a complaint or an investigation. Nothing here counts violations that occurred, and a falling line is not evidence of improving compliance — it is equally consistent with fewer investigations, or with fewer complaints from workers whose right to remain depends on the employer they would be complaining about. This data cannot say which, and the distinction should not be blurred in either direction. Back wages are “agreed to pay”, a finding rather than money proven collected.
