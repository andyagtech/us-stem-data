Nativity is not visa status. “Foreign born” here pools naturalised citizens, permanent residents and temporary-visa holders together, and “native born” means born in the United States whatever the parentage. It is a far broader cut than the visa categories used elsewhere in this document and the two are not the same population. The earnings figures are equally not a like-for-like comparison: the groups differ in age, education, occupation, region and language, none of which is controlled here, so the gap describes two populations rather than what an employer pays for the same work.
