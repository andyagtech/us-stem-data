8. The biggest filers

Who files under the exemption, as far as the published data allows

No source records cap status against a named employer. The closest available measure is petitions approved for employers classified under educational services — 2,970 of them in FY2025, accounting for 24,593 approvals.

Largest educational-services H-1B filers, FY2025, petitions approved. NAICS 61. Source: USCIS H-1B Employer Data Hub.

This is a proxy, not a count of cap-exempt petitions. The classification includes school districts and for-profit schools, which are not cap-exempt, and excludes nonprofits affiliated with a university, which file under other codes. Mayo Clinic and Dallas Independent School District appear above for that reason.

The affiliated-nonprofit route, by name

Open Avenues Foundation, which describes itself as the first nonprofit affiliated with U.S. colleges and universities to offer cap-exempt sponsorship, went from 2 approvals in FY2019 to 79 in FY2025. It files under NAICS 81, Other Services — not under education.

That single organisation is what a name search finds, not what a census would. The 24,073 affiliated-nonprofit petitions on the previous page are a national total; the organisations composing it are not published, and cannot be assembled from any source listed here.
