Open Avenues Foundation

The organisation behind the Build Fellowship, and the clearest public example of the affiliated-nonprofit route. Revenue rose from $279,566 in FY2018 to $4,244,093 in FY2023. Its H-1B approvals over a comparable period: FY2019 2, FY2020 9, FY2021 39, FY2022 53, FY2023 27, FY2024 118, FY2025 79.

Revenue is not a count of sponsorships, and a 990 records no cap status. The organisations above file H-1B petitions at volume and fall within the statutory cap-exempt employer types; no published source confirms cap status for any named employer.
