9. Where it concentrates

In several fields, U.S. citizens are a minority of doctorates awarded

The national doctorate figure of 38.8% is an average across very different fields. The tables below pool every institution that reports to IPEDS — not a sample, and not the institutions this project tracks individually. Totals are for 2021–2025.
