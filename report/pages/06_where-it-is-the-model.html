5. Where it is the model

At some institutions the science and engineering master’s is almost entirely a foreign-student programme

The national figures on page {{page:balance}} are averages, and averages hide the institutions that have built a programme around this. Of full-time science, engineering and health graduate enrolment, 46.8% at master’s level and 44.5% at doctoral level is held by temporary-visa holders. At the institutions below the master’s figure is above ninety.
