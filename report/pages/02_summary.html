Summary · one

Five findings, each from a federal source

1 · The shift is at master’s level, and it has not turned

Non-residents took 24.4% of core-STEM master’s degrees in 1995 and 45.9% in 2025. The doctorate moved +4.8 points over the same thirty years, peaked in 2021 and has fallen since. American output rose at both levels — master’s +189% — so this is a change in share, not an American retreat. IPEDS Completions. Page {{page:balance}}.

2 · The federally funded route is the one Americans take least

Research assistantships are how federal money reaches doctoral students: 52,428 federally supported research assistantships against 1,041 teaching ones. They fund 48.9% of visa holders against 29.3% of citizens — a gap of 19.6 points that has not fallen below 19.3 in any of the seven years on record. NSF SED and GSS. Pages {{page:whopays}}, {{page:supporttrend}}.

3 · The money and the enrolment are in the same institutions

Across the 99 institutions awarding at least a hundred core-STEM research doctorates, federal obligations run to a median of $874,630 for every research doctorate awarded — the scale of public research money moving through them. And at 108 institutions a majority of graduate students hold a temporary visa: those hold $19.4bn of federal research expenditure, 32.9% of the matched total, and 210,870 graduate students. A second model sits beside it: at the highest-share master’s programmes there is almost no federal research money and $3.36bn a year in published tuition billed to non-resident master’s graduates. NSF Federal Support Survey, HERD and GSS; IPEDS. An intensity ratio, not a price — those obligations fund research, not only degrees. Pages {{page:whopays}}, {{page:funding}}, {{page:tuitionmodel}}.

4 · The work visa is cheaper twice over

First on the wage: 84.0% of the 517,103 H-1B filings that can be placed promise less than the median wage for their occupation in the metropolitan area where the work is. For software developers the four legal wage levels run 63%, 74%, 91% and 105% of that median — three of the four sit below it. Then on the tax: wages earned on Optional Practical Training carry no Social Security or Medicare, saving the employer 7.65% of payroll across 289,938 authorisations. DOL OFLC and BLS OEWS state file; IRC 3121(b)(19); DHS SEVP. Pages {{page:wagelevels}}, {{page:fica}}.

5 · The student visa is the front of a route to permanent residence

58.3% of those who earned a U.S. doctorate on a temporary visa now hold citizenship or a green card; only 9.8% remain on a temporary visa. Nationally, 74.6% of employment-based green cards go to people already in the country, against 5.4% on the family route. NSF SDR; DHS Yearbook. Pages {{page:transition}}, {{page:adjust}}.
