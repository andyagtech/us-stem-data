5. The route to a green card

The employment green card is not a way to bring someone in. It is overwhelmingly a way to convert someone already here

The contrast is the finding. Family-sponsored admission and the diversity lottery bring people in from outside; employment-based admission mostly changes the status of people already living and working here. It has run between 74.6% and 91.6% for the decade.

An adjustment shows the person was already in the country. It does not record which status they held before, so this table cannot say they studied here. The labour-certification figures beside it do carry that field, and they point the same way. Counts are persons, not degrees.
