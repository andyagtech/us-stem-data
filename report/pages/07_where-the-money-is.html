The federal research money and the foreign enrolment are in the same institutions

Where the two meet

At 106 institutions a majority of doctoral students hold a temporary visa. Those institutions account for $12.5bn of federal R&D expenditure — 21.5% of the matched total — and 70,197 doctoral students.

This page is about doctoral students, deliberately. Federal research money pays research assistantships and the graduate survey shows master’s students are overwhelmingly unfunded, so a master’s share says nothing about where grant money goes. HERD reports R&D expenditure, not all federal money and not student support, and it is recorded at the institution — nothing here traces a dollar to a named student. 172 institutions could not be matched by name; unmatched does not mean unfunded.
