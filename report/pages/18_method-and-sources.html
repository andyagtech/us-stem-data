12. Method and sources

How these figures were produced

Method

Degree counts are from IPEDS Completions, restricted to eight core-STEM CIP families (11, 14, 15, 26, 27, 30, 40, 42), using award levels 7 for master's and 17 for research doctorates. Domestic counts are derived as total minus non-resident. Doctorate citizenship figures for all fields come from the NSF Survey of Earned Doctorates, a census rather than a sample. Citizenship detail is unavailable in IPEDS before 1995; 1990 and 1999 are not published by NCES.

Sources

NCES IPEDS Completions, 1995–2025 · nces.ed.gov/ipeds
NSF NCSES Survey of Earned Doctorates, 1979–2024, Tables 1-7 and 4-1 · ncses.nsf.gov
NSF NCSES Federal Support Survey, NSF 25-339 Table 17, FY2023 · ncses.nsf.gov
DHS Student and Exchange Visitor Program, OPT/STEM OPT/CPT authorisations 2007–2024 and the 2024 Top 200 employers · ice.gov/sevis
U.S. State Department, Nonimmigrant Visas Issued by Classification, FY2005–FY2024 · travel.state.gov
USCIS, I-129 petition approvals, the STEM-Related Petition Trends factsheet, and the annual Report on H-1B Petitions to Congress FY2018–FY2025 · uscis.gov
U.S. Department of Labor, Office of Foreign Labor Certification, LCA disclosure files, FY2008–FY2026 · dol.gov/agencies/eta/foreign-labor/performance

The underlying figures, the code that produces them, and the per-institution detail behind every table are published at phd-demographics.andy-barr.com. Every chart here can be reproduced from the sources above.

Per-institution pages, the national series behind each chart, a SQL query interface over the underlying records, and a machine-readable catalogue are all published there.
