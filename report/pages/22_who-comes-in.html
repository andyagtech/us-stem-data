8. Who comes in

The intracompany route did not grow. The specialty-worker and professional routes did

The visa counts elsewhere in this document are documents issued at a consulate. These are arrivals: admissions recorded at a port of entry, which is the measure that shows whether a route is actually being used.

Where they arrive is the closest thing to an employer signal these classes have. L-1 admissions concentrate in Texas 73,350, California 62,580, New York 62,070, Michigan 42,080 — Michigan alongside the usual three is the automotive pattern. O-1 concentrates in New York 31,260, California 30,200, Florida 11,200.

An admission is an entry at a port, not a person and not a visa. One worker travelling four times in a year is four admissions, which is why 2023 shows 755,020 H-1B admissions against 265,777 H-1B issuances. The two series must never be mixed, and neither counts workers. There is no employer-level data at all for L-1 or O-1 — page {{page:dataneeds2}} sets out what would be needed.
