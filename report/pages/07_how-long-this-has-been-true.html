5. How long this has been true

The funding gap on page {{page:whopays}} is not new, and it has not widened. It has held for seven straight years

A single year cannot say whether a gap is recent or entrenched. The Survey of Earned Doctorates publishes the same question with every release, so the series below is assembled from seven of them, 2018 through 2024.

Two things the chart does not say. First, the SED records that a student was funded by a research assistantship, not which award paid for it; the federal link comes from the Survey of Graduate Students and Postdoctorates, which shows federal support reaching doctoral students overwhelmingly through research assistantships — 52,428 federally supported RAs against 1,041 federally supported teaching assistantships. Second, both lines rise together: the citizen share is up 2.9 points over the period and the visa share 2.5. The finding here is duration and scale, not deterioration. The series runs on all fields because NCSES revised the field taxonomy in 2023, which would otherwise split a science-and-engineering series across two definitions.
