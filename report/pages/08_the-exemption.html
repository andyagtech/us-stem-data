7. The exemption

The cap does not apply to universities — or to nonprofits affiliated with them

Three employer types are exempt from the H-1B numerical cap by statute: institutions of higher education, nonprofits related to or affiliated with one, and nonprofit or government research organisations. They file without entering the lottery.

Petitions from the affiliated-nonprofit category rose 124% between 2018 and 2025, from 10,734 to 24,073. Universities themselves rose 38%.
