10. What was promised

Two in three H-1B filings name the bottom of the legal wage range

Every H-1B filing states a prevailing-wage level for the occupation, from I to IV. Level I is the entry rate, Level IV the experienced one. Across 1,150,107 certified filings in 2019 and 2023, 64.6% named Level I or Level II.

Filing at Level I or II is entirely lawful. It is the cheapest legal way to fill a role, and this page reports what employers promised, not what they paid. The obvious next question — whether low wage levels predict later violations — cannot be answered here: only six of these employers appear in the published enforcement extract, which carries just its largest cases, and enforcement is not random to begin with, since large employers and staffing firms are investigated more and both skew toward low levels. Six matches cannot support a correlation, and none is claimed.
