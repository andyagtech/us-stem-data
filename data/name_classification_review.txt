METHODOLOGY & ERROR ESTIMATES
==========================================================================================

Approach: Surname-based matching against 90 Chinese surnames.

ERROR SOURCES:

1. FALSE POSITIVES (we count as Chinese but aren't):
   - Ambiguous surnames shared with Korean/Vietnamese/other: ~11 surnames
   - Estimated false positive rate: 5-10%
   - Worst offenders: Lin, Ma, Han, Yu, Tang

2. FALSE NEGATIVES (Chinese but we miss them):
   - Excluded 'lee', 'long', 'sun' — probably miss ~2-3% of real Chinese names
   - Missing uncommon surnames (qiao, zhong, xia, etc.) — miss ~1-2%
   - Adopted Western surnames (marriage, etc.) — unknowable
   - Total estimated false negative rate: 5-8%

3. NET EFFECT: Errors roughly cancel out. Overall counts likely
   within +/- 5% of truth.

POTENTIAL IMPROVEMENTS:
   - Use given name + surname together (e.g., "Wang, Xiaoming" = very likely Chinese)
   - Use a trained classifier like ethnicolr or NamePrism
   - Cross-reference with known Chinese given name databases
   - Manual review of ambiguous cases (this document)
