# DOL Wage and Hour Division enforcement (WHISARD) — where to put the files

Save the downloaded files here with their original names.

Sources:
- Data catalog: https://enforcedata.dol.gov/views/data_catalogs.php
- Summaries and direct CSVs: https://enforcedata.dol.gov/views/data_summary.php
- H-1B debarred/violator list: https://www.dol.gov/agencies/whd/immigration/h1b

## What is wanted

The **WHD compliance action** dataset (`whd_whisard`), which carries one row
per investigation with the employer, the act cited, back wages found and
employees affected. Filter to the H-1B provisions of the INA.

## Why it matters here

This is the only source in reach that reaches the H-1B population the project
otherwise cannot see. Every survey used here covers doctorate holders; most
H-1B workers hold a bachelor's or master's and work in industry. WHISARD
records findings against employers of those workers.

Read it for what it is: enforcement records show violations that were
**found**, which depends on complaints filed and investigations opened. A low
count is not evidence of compliance, and this is a floor, never a rate.
