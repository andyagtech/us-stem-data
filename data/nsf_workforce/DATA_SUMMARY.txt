================================================================================
1. SURVEY OF DOCTORATE RECIPIENTS (SDR) - PUBLIC USE MICRODATA
================================================================================

Files: sdr_2023/ and sdr_2021/
Source: https://ncses.nsf.gov/explore-data/microdata/doctorate-recipients

SDR 2023 (sdr_2023/psd23Public/):
  - epsd23.csv           CSV microdata (80,143 records)
  - epsd23.sas7bdat      SAS format microdata
  - EPSD23.DAT           ASCII fixed-width data
  - Ppsd23.pdf           Variable codebook
  - Dpsd23.xlsx          Data dictionary crosswalk
  - psd23_Annotated.pdf  Survey instrument
  - LAYOUTPSD23.TXT      ASCII layout file
  - Fpsd23.sas, Lpsd23.sas, Ppsd23.sas  SAS programs (formats, labels, proc)

Key variables for China/foreign-born analysis:
  - BTHUS: Born in U.S. (Y/N)
  - CTZN: Citizenship status
  - CTZFOR: Foreign citizenship
  - CTZUS: U.S. citizenship
  - CTZUSIN: U.S. citizenship by naturalization
  - CTZN_DRF: Citizenship at time of doctorate

SDR 2021 also available in sdr_2021/ with same structure.

================================================================================
2. NATIONAL SURVEY OF COLLEGE GRADUATES (NSCG) - PUBLIC USE MICRODATA
================================================================================

Files: nscg_2023/ and nscg_2021/
Source: https://ncses.nsf.gov/explore-data/microdata/national-survey-college-graduates

NSCG 2023 (nscg_2023/pcg23Public/):
  - epcg23.csv           CSV microdata (94,606 records)
  - epcg23.sas7bdat      SAS format microdata
  - EPCG23.DAT           ASCII fixed-width data
  - Ppcg23.pdf           Variable codebook
  - Dpcg23.xlsx          Data dictionary crosswalk
  - 2023-NSCG-21_annotated_7Aug25.pdf  Survey instrument
  - 2023NSCG_RecodeDocumentation_4Feb25.pdf  Recode documentation
  - TOGA_crosswalk_NSCG23_20250116.xlsx  TOGA crosswalk
  - LAYOUTPCG23.TXT      ASCII layout file

Key variables for China/foreign-born analysis:
  - BTHUS: Born in U.S. (Y/N)
  - BTHRGN: Birth region (more geographic detail)
  - BTHST_TOGA: Birth state/country code (specific country identification)
  - CTZN: Citizenship status
  - CTZFOR: Foreign citizenship
  - CTZUS: U.S. citizenship
  - CTZUSIN: U.S. citizenship by naturalization
  - CTZDUAL: Dual citizenship

NOTE: NSCG has more granular birth country data than SDR (BTHRGN + BTHST_TOGA
vs. just BTHUS). This makes NSCG better for country-specific analysis (e.g., China).

NSCG 2021 also available in nscg_2021/ with same structure.

4. NSB 2024 REPORT: STEM LABOR FORCE (SUPPLEMENTAL TABLES)
================================================================================

Files: nsb_foreign_born_stem/
Source: https://ncses.nsf.gov/pubs/nsb20245/ (Science & Engineering Indicators 2024)

28 Excel tables + supplemental PDF. Key tables for foreign-born analysis:
  - Table SLBR-25: Employed STEM workers by FOREIGN-BORN and CITIZENSHIP STATUS
  - Table SLBR-26: TOP COUNTRY OF BIRTH for foreign-born STEM workers by occupation
  - Table SLBR-27: Citizenship of workers with bachelor's+ by STEM occupation
  - Table SLBR-28: POSTGRADUATION PLANS of U.S. doctorates on temporary visas
                    BY COUNTRY OF ORIGIN (includes China), 2010-2021

Key findings from this report:
  - Mexico: 18% of foreign-born STEM workers
  - India: 14% of foreign-born STEM workers
  - China: 7% of foreign-born STEM workers
  - Philippines: 6% of foreign-born STEM workers
  - S&E occupations specifically: India 29%, China 13%

================================================================================
ANALYSIS NOTES
================================================================================

For China-specific S&E workforce analysis, the most useful datasets are:

1. NSCG 2023 microdata (epcg23.csv) - Has BTHST_TOGA for country-level birth
   identification. Can cross-tabulate with occupation, field, salary, employer.
   94,606 individual records with survey weights.

2. NSB 2026 Table TAL-5 and TAL-6 - Pre-built country-of-origin tables for
   foreign-born STEM workers (2023 ACS data).

3. NSB 2026 Table TAL-17 - Time series of doctoral stay rates by country of
   origin, useful for tracking China trends over 2012-2023.

4. NSB 2024 Table SLBR-26 and SLBR-28 - Country-level breakdowns from prior
   edition (2021 data), useful for comparison.

5. SDR 2023 tables (Table 9, 25, 39) - Citizenship breakdowns for doctoral
   workforce, though without country-of-birth specificity.
