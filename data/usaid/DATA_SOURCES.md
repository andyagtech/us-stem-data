## 1. USAID Scholarship Programs

### 1.1 Higher Education Solutions Network (HESN)

- **What**: Partnership between USAID and 7 top universities (MIT, UC Berkeley, Michigan State, Duke, Texas A&M, William & Mary, Makerere University Uganda)
- **Scale**: $115 million invested across 8 Development Labs; 400+ partner institutions in 60+ countries
- **Output**: 900+ products/technologies addressing poverty-related challenges
- **Status**: Launched 2012; archived content at https://2017-2020.usaid.gov/hesn
- **Impact report**: https://www.edu-links.org/resources/impact-report-higher-education-solutions-network
- **Evaluation**: Mathematica performed a formal performance evaluation: https://www.mathematica.org/projects/performance-evaluation-of-the-usaid-higher-education-solutions-network

### 1.2 USAID University Partnerships (Broader)

- **Scale**: At least 139 US colleges/universities and 174 HEIs overseas
- **In 2023**: USAID collaborated with 830+ HEIs, producing 690 peer-reviewed papers, reaching ~100,000 students
- **Programs within this umbrella**:
  - **Partnerships for Enhanced Engagement in Research (PEER)**: Funds scientists/engineers in developing countries collaborating with US-funded researchers (NSF, NIH)
  - **Feed the Future Innovation Labs**: 500+ collaborating institutions, 55+ host countries
- **URL**: https://www.usaid.gov/partnership-opportunities/universities

### 1.3 Country-Specific USAID Scholarship Programs

#### Pakistan
- **Merit and Needs-Based Scholarship Program** (with HEC Pakistan)
- Phase 2 (2013): $23.1 million, 3,000 scholarships, 50% for female students, across 30 partner universities
- Covers: tuition, accommodation, textbooks, food allowance
- For bachelor's and master's degrees at Pakistani universities
- Total Pakistan USAID portfolio: $845 million across 39 projects (suspended 2025)
- **URL**: https://www.hec.gov.pk/english/scholarshipsgrants/USAID-NeedsBased/Pages/default.aspx

#### Egypt
- USAID Scholars at American University in Cairo (AUC)
- ~1,077 students enrolled in public and private universities funded by USAID
- Egypt's Ministry of Higher Education stepped in to cover costs after USAID freeze
- **URL**: https://www.aucegypt.edu/admissions/scholarships/USAIDscholars

#### Myanmar / Southeast Asia
- $45 million in USAID funding for 400+ doctoral/postgraduate students (frozen 2025)

#### Indonesia
- $5 million USAID award (terminated March 2025) had helped Indonesian International Education Foundation secure $26 million in 2024 to send Indonesians to American universities

#### Kenya
- USAID Higher Education Scholarship Programme (documented as of 2010)

### 1.4 USAID Payne Fellowship
- Donald M. Payne International Development Graduate Fellowship
- Up to $104,000 in benefits over 2 years
- Pathway to USAID Foreign Service
- **US residents only** (not for foreign nationals)

### 1.5 Development and Inclusive Scholarship Program (DISP)
- Country-specific programs for students with disabilities and underrepresented groups
- **URL**: https://www.usaiddisp.com/

## 2. Fulbright Program (State Dept / IIE)

### 2.1 Scale
- **Total grants annually**: ~8,000
  - ~4,000 to foreign students coming to the US (Fulbright Foreign Student Program)
  - ~1,600 to US students going abroad
  - ~1,200 to US scholars
  - ~850 to foreign scholars coming to the US
- **Countries**: 160+
- **Since inception (1946)**: 400,000+ participants

### 2.2 Statistics by Country (US Student Program)
- Germany: ~91 awards/year (most of any country)
- India: ~55 awards/year
- United Kingdom: ~47 awards/year
- Mexico: ~29 awards/year
- ~4,000+ applications per year for Study/Research Grants (4,166 in 2023-2024 cycle)
- Europe & Eurasia: 62% of applications

### 2.3 Data Availability
- **US Student statistics**: https://us.fulbrightonline.org/statistics
- **Study/Research & ETA stats**: https://us.fulbrightonline.org/study-research-eta-statistics
- **Awards by country**: https://us.fulbrightonline.org/countries
- **Foreign Student Program**: https://foreign.fulbrightonline.org/about/foreign-student-program
- **Fulbright Scholar facts**: https://fulbrightscholars.org/what-fulbright/fulbright-facts
- **Program details by country (State Dept)**: https://eca.state.gov/fulbright/fulbright-programs/program-details-country
- **Acceptance rates by country**: https://worldpopulationreview.com/country-rankings/fulbright-acceptance-rate-by-country
- **Top producing institutions**: https://fulbrightprogram.org/fulbright-top-producing-institutions/

### 2.4 Downloadability
- Statistics pages show 3-year rolling data by country
- Full historical datasets may require contacting IIE or State Dept directly
- Open Doors data (which includes Fulbright) has downloadable Excel files

## 3. Other US Government Exchange/Fellowship Programs

### 3.1 Humphrey Fellowship Program
- **Agency**: State Dept (administered by IIE)
- **Annual participants**: ~150-200
- **Total since 1978**: 5,600+
- Non-degree program for mid-career professionals from developing countries
- 10 months at a US university

### 3.2 International Visitor Leadership Program (IVLP)
- **Agency**: State Dept Bureau of Educational and Cultural Affairs
- **Annual participants**: ~4,000
- **Total since 1940**: 230,000+
- **Notable alumni**: 500+ heads of state/government, 12 Nobel Laureates, 1,600+ Cabinet Ministers
- Short-term professional exchanges (not degree programs)
- **URL**: https://exchanges.state.gov/non-us/program/international-visitor-leadership-program-ivlp

### 3.3 Cochran Fellowship Program
- **Agency**: USDA Foreign Agricultural Service
- **Duration**: 2-3 weeks of training
- **Focus**: Agricultural professionals from middle-income countries
- Since 1984: 1,330 participants from sub-Saharan Africa alone
- **URL**: https://www.fas.usda.gov/programs/cochran-fellowship-program

### 3.4 Borlaug Fellowship Program
- **Agency**: USDA Foreign Agricultural Service
- **Duration**: 8-12 weeks
- **Total since 2004**: ~900 fellows from 69 countries
- One-on-one mentorship at US universities/research centers
- **URL**: https://www.fas.usda.gov/programs/borlaug-fellowship-program

### 3.5 Global Undergraduate Exchange Program (Global UGRAD)
- **Agency**: State Dept
- For undergraduates from underrepresented backgrounds in developing countries
- One semester at a US university (non-degree)
- **URL**: https://exchanges.state.gov/non-us/program/global-undergraduate-exchange-program-global-ugrad

## 4. Bureau of Educational and Cultural Affairs (ECA) - Umbrella

- **FY2024 budget**: $779.5 million
- **FY2025 requested**: $808.6 million (8.8% increase)
- **FY2025 actual**: Subject to OMB cuts; at least 22 ECA programs targeted for cuts
- Administers: Fulbright, IVLP, Humphrey, Critical Language Scholarship, Youth Exchange, and many more
- **Budget data**: https://www.usaspending.gov/federal_account/019-0209
- **NAFSA tracking**: https://www.nafsa.org/issue-brief-fy2025-funding-international-education-and-exchange-programs

## 6. Key Question: Government-Funded vs Self-Funded International Students

### Answer (from IIE Open Doors 2024/2025)

**Total international students in US (2024/25)**: 1,177,766 (1.2 million)

**Primary funding sources**:
| Source | Approximate Share |
|--------|------------------|
| Personal & family funds | ~52% |
| US college/university (assistantships, scholarships) | ~26% |
| Home government/university | ~6% |
| **US government** | **~0.8-2%** |
| Other sources | ~14% |

**Key finding**: Only about 2% of all international students receive ANY US government funding. The vast majority (52%) are primarily self/family-funded. US institutional aid (assistantships, tuition waivers) is the second largest source at ~26%.

**In absolute numbers**:
- ~1.18 million total international students
- ~10,000-24,000 receive US government funding as primary source
- This includes Fulbright (~4,000 foreign students/year), Humphrey (~200), IVLP (~4,000 short-term), plus various USAID country programs

**Economic contribution**: International students contributed ~$55 billion to the US economy in 2024, supporting 355,000+ jobs.

## 7. Impact of 2025 USAID Shutdown

The Trump administration's effective dismantlement of USAID in early 2025 has severely impacted education programs:

- **Pakistan**: $845M in projects suspended, including women's and flood-affected student scholarships
- **Myanmar**: $45M frozen affecting 400+ doctoral/postgraduate students
- **Egypt**: 1,077 USAID-funded students affected; Egyptian government stepped in temporarily
- **Indonesia**: $5M award terminated
- **Global**: USAID had been collaborating with 830+ HEIs worldwide
- **DEC taken offline**: 200,000+ documents no longer publicly accessible (archived by third parties)
- **ForeignAssistance.gov**: Data preservation efforts underway (Andrew Heiss backup)
