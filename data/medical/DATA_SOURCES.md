## Data Not Downloaded (Requires Manual Action)

### AAMC Citizenship/Visa Status Data
The AAMC FACTS tables available for public download do not include breakdowns by citizenship status or country of origin. This data exists but may require:
- A data request to datarequest@aamc.org
- Access through AAMC's restricted datasets
- The AAMC "Medical School Enrollment Survey" which tracks non-US citizens/permanent residents

### Additional AAMC FACTS Tables Available
Many more tables exist at these URLs (not all downloaded):
- Applicants & Matriculants: https://www.aamc.org/data-reports/students-residents/data/facts-applicants-and-matriculants
- Enrollment & Graduates: https://www.aamc.org/data-reports/students-residents/data/facts-enrollment-graduates-and-md-phd
- ERAS (residency applications): https://www.aamc.org/data-reports/students-residents/data/2025-facts-electronic-residency-application-service-eras-data

Key tables not yet downloaded that may be relevant:
- A-3/A-4: Applicants/matriculants by state (2016-2026)
- A-10/A-11: Applicants/matriculants by race/ethnicity and state
- B-4: Graduates by race/ethnicity and gender
- ERAS tables (residency application data)

### NRMP Additional Reports
The NRMP publishes additional data that may require purchase or account access:
- Results and Data reports (historical match results)
- Main Residency Match data by specialty and applicant type
- Available at https://www.nrmp.org/match-data-analytics/residency-data-reports/

### ECFMG Annual Report
ECFMG publishes an annual report with detailed certification statistics:
- Historical data at https://www.ecfmg.org/resources/ECFMG-annual-report.html (may redirect to Intealth)

### Other Potential Sources
- **AMA Physician Masterfile**: Tracks all physicians including IMGs; restricted access
- **AAMC Physician Workforce data**: https://www.aamc.org/data-reports/workforce
- **GME Track**: Residency program data from AAMC
- **OPTN/HRSA**: Organ procurement data that tracks physician demographics
