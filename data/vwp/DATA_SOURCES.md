### Key Finding

VWP countries account for ~67,680 naturalizations per year (7.7% of total). South Korea alone accounts for 12,330 (18% of VWP total). The top 5 VWP naturalizers (South Korea, UK, Poland, Taiwan, France) account for ~56% of all VWP naturalizations.

For comparison, the top 5 overall naturalizing countries are:
1. Mexico: ~115,000 (13.1%)
2. India: ~53,600 (6.1%)
3. Philippines: ~43,900 (5.0%)
4. Dominican Republic: ~43,000 (4.9%)
5. Vietnam: ~36,000 (4.1%)

## 7. Key Findings

### Are VWP citizens naturalizing at significant rates?

**Moderately.** VWP countries produce ~67,680 naturalizations per year, or 7.7% of the US total. This is notable but not dominant compared to countries like Mexico (115K), India (54K), or the Philippines (44K).

### Which VWP countries have the most immigration to the US?

**By naturalization volume:**
1. South Korea (12,330)
2. United Kingdom (10,990)
3. Poland (5,650)
4. Taiwan (4,800)
5. France (4,010)

**By green card volume:**
1. South Korea (15,770)
2. United Kingdom (9,720)
3. Taiwan (5,300)
4. Germany (4,550)
5. France (4,480)

**By nonimmigrant travel volume:**
1. United Kingdom (4.0M departures)
2. Germany (1.9M)
3. France (1.9M)
4. Japan (1.5M)
5. Italy (1.2M)

**By student population:**
1. South Korea (61,277)
2. Taiwan (28,935)
3. Japan (26,435)
4. United Kingdom (16,611)
5. Germany (15,513)

### South Korea and Taiwan are outliers

Among VWP countries, South Korea and Taiwan stand out with disproportionately high immigration (naturalization, LPR, students) relative to their travel volumes. South Korea alone accounts for 18% of all VWP naturalizations and has 61K students in the US.

### Overstay compliance is high

VWP countries have a 0.43% overstay rate vs 2.22% for non-VWP countries. Only Chile (2.32%) exceeds the 2% threshold. Japan has the lowest rate at 0.10%.
