KEY FINDINGS
===============================================================================================

HIGHEST CHINESE REPRESENTATION (UC STEM):
  1. Statistics:            44.6% — nearly half of all dissertations
  2. Electrical Engineering: 33.3%
  3. Materials Science:     32.9%
  4. Computer Science:      30.6%
  5. Chemical Engineering:  25.6%

LOWEST (among major STEM fields):
  1. Ecology/Earth Sci:      7.0%
  2. Astronomy:              7.8%
  3. Genetics:               8.5%
  4. Public Health:          9.1%
  5. Neuroscience:          11.6%

FASTEST GROWING (2010-14 to 2020-25):
  - Materials Science: 20.7% → 37.0%  (+16.3 pts)
  - Mathematics:       12.9% → 27.3%  (+14.4 pts)
  - Computer Science:  23.9% → 34.8%  (+10.9 pts)
  - Statistics:        37.9% → 47.4%  (+9.5 pts)
  - Physics:           16.0% → 24.3%  (+8.3 pts)

CROSS-ETHNICITY PATTERNS:
  - Korean: Uniform 1-4% across fields; highest in Materials Sci & Chem Eng
  - Indian: Concentrated in CS (2.7%) & engineering; very low in phys. sci.
  - Vietnamese: Highest in Aerospace (3.3%), Biomed Eng (3.1%), Biology (2.4%)

UC vs STANFORD:
  Chinese % consistently higher at UC across all fields.
  Gap largest in Statistics (~45% vs ~30%) and Materials Sci (~33% vs ~26%).
  Likely reflects UC's larger international student enrollment.
