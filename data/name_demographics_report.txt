5. KEY FINDINGS
===========================================================================

• Chinese-likely names comprise 19-21% of UC STEM dissertations (2015-2025),
  rising from ~14% in 2010 to ~24% in 2025.

• UCLA leads at 26-29% Chinese-likely, followed by UC Riverside (24-26%)
  and UC San Diego (21-24%). UC Berkeley is lower at 15-16%.

• Stanford is at 15-17%, somewhat below UC average.

• Portland State has only 2.4-3.1% Chinese-likely STEM PhDs — roughly 
  7-8x lower than UC campuses. This reflects PSU's regional teaching
  focus vs. R1 research institutions.

• PSU peaked in the 1990s (~9% Chinese STEM PhDs) and has since declined,
  while UC and Stanford have steadily increased.

• Confidence bands are narrow (2-3 percentage points), suggesting the
  surname-based approach is reasonably robust despite ambiguity.

METHODOLOGY:
  - 148 Chinese surnames (pinyin + alt romanizations)
  - 395 standard Mandarin pinyin syllables for given-name validation
  - Ambiguous surnames (lin, yu, han, ma, etc.) included with confidence flag
  - Excluded: lee, long, sun (too common as Western/Korean surnames)
  - High confidence = unambiguous surname OR ambiguous + Chinese given name
