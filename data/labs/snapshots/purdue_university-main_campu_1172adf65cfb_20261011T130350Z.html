Weldon School of Biomedical Engineering

West Lafayette and Indianapolis
Biomedical Engineers innovate medical devices, advanced therapeutics and better healthcare diagnostics and delivery systems. At Purdue, we create exciting new spaces for experiential learning, clinical collaborations and discovery with global impact.
